Profile

About

My work centres on climate change, natural hazards and spatial analysis, with geospatial science providing the common framework across diverse environmental research problems.

I am an Environmental Geographer and Geospatial Researcher with research interests spanning climate change, natural hazards, environmental systems and spatial analysis. My doctoral research focused on Himalayan lake ecosystems, phytoplankton dynamics and climate-related environmental change.

A central part of my work is the application of geospatial technologies to understand complex environmental and spatial processes. I use GIS, satellite remote sensing, spatial modelling and statistical approaches together with artificial intelligence, machine learning and deep learning where appropriate.

My research has addressed diverse problems including landslide susceptibility, forest-fire risk, land-surface-temperature change, groundwater mapping, carbon sequestration, ecological change and climate-sensitive Himalayan environments.

My broader interests include climate-risk assessment, multi-hazard analysis, geospatial AI, remote-sensing applications and the development of spatial tools and data-driven systems for environmental research. Environmental monitoring, field observations, sensors and citizen-generated data form part of this broader geospatial research framework where relevant.

Founder · Citizen Science · Geospatial Platform

HazWatch

A citizen-powered multi-hazard reporting and geospatial mapping platform for India. HazWatch supports current and retrospective hazard reporting, GPS and map-based incident documentation, field photographs, hazard visualisation and controlled access to anonymised research datasets.
