About

A research profile built around environmental systems, spatial intelligence and real-world hazard problems.

I am an Environmental Geographer and Geospatial Researcher with a PhD focused on Himalayan lake ecosystems, phytoplankton and climate-change impacts.

My work combines GIS, remote sensing, environmental monitoring, statistical analysis and machine learning to understand climate-sensitive landscapes and natural hazards.

My research experience includes work at IIT Roorkee on AI-based crop-health monitoring using satellite, UAV and PhenoCam observations, and postdoctoral research on climate-change impacts on landslides in Nainital.

Current interests include multi-hazard risk, Himalayan environments, climate adaptation, citizen science and geospatial data infrastructure.
