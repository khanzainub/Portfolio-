Research approach

About

Research questions guide my work; analytical methods and practical tools help me investigate and communicate them.

I am an Environmental Geography researcher with doctoral training and postdoctoral experience at Aligarh Muslim University and IIT Roorkee. My work brings together field observations, spatial datasets and modelling to understand environmental processes and support evidence-based decisions.

Recently, I have extended this approach into research applications: interactive models, simulators and tools for exploring environmental information. These projects grow from my research practice, with an emphasis on clear assumptions, validation and useful communication of results.

Research in practice

Selected Projects

Applications of spatial analysis and environmental modelling: from participatory hazard documentation to simulation, lake-temperature reconstruction and everyday environmental decisions.

01 · Citizen science & disaster documentation

HazWatch

Available on Google Play

An Android application that enables people to document current and past hazard events with locations and photographs. It connects citizen participation with geospatial hazard mapping and organised, anonymised information for research and public awareness.

02 · Spatial simulation

Wildfire Spread Simulator

In progress

A browser-based research simulator using cellular automata to explore wildfire spread under changing environmental conditions. It combines satellite and weather data with observed fire information, calibration and validation workflows.

Cellular automataRemote sensingHazard modelling

03 · Environmental modelling

Thermal-Memory Lake Temperature Model

Research prototype

A web tool for reconstructing and predicting lake surface temperature from atmospheric forcing and sparse lake observations. A lake-specific thermal-memory formulation supports calibration, independent validation and gap filling.
