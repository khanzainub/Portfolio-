About

I am an Environmental Geography researcher with a PhD and postdoctoral research experience. My work explores Himalayan hazards, aquatic ecosystems, and climate-driven environmental change through an integrated approach combining field evidence, remote sensing, GIS, and environmental modelling.

Bridging scientific inquiry and practical innovation, I develop geospatial applications and modelling tools that transform complex environmental data into meaningful insights. My research is grounded in methodological rigour, analytical transparency, robust validation, and a commitment to advancing evidence-based environmental decision-making.

Environmental modelling

Thermal-Memory Lake Temperature Model

Research prototype

A web tool for reconstructing and predicting lake surface temperature from atmospheric forcing and sparse lake observations. A lake-specific thermal-memory formulation supports calibration, independent validation and gap filling. Independently conceptualised, designed, developed and maintained.

Spatial forecasting · QGIS plugin

SpatialForecast

QGIS plugin

A QGIS plugin for forecasting raster time series across individual pixels or spatial blocks. It aligns raster grids and offers eight forecasting models, validation diagnostics, forecast maps and publication-quality figures. Independently conceptualised and maintained; co-designed and co-developed with Siddhartha Khare.
