How to read this explorer · sources and limitations

Source: World Bank World Development Indicators, using total population (SP.POP.TOTL), male population (SP.POP.TOTL.MA.IN) and female population (SP.POP.TOTL.FE.IN). Underlying sources include the UN Population Division and national statistical agencies. These are annual demographic estimates, not a live population counter.

Countries and economies are shown separately from World Bank regional/income aggregates. World summary cards use the published world aggregate, not a sum of the visible bubbles. Region filters affect bubbles and rankings only. Missing data stay missing; no zero values are invented. Countries need both male and female estimates to appear in the bubble plot.

Between years, bubbles interpolate adjacent annual observations for smooth motion. The slider label, cards, ranking and downloadable CSV use the displayed whole year. Bubble positions are statistical, not geographical. Population uses a logarithmic horizontal axis so small and large countries remain visible; circle area uses a fixed population scale across years. Select a small country from the dropdown if its bubble is hard to tap.

This country-level series is separate from the local WorldPop estimates on WeatherScope’s main page. Different sources and revisions may differ. Male/female are the source dataset’s sex categories. No future projections have been appended.
